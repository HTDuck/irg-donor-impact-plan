Medical Section

Field medicine in affected areas.

03

What we do

IRG's Medical Section provides immediate, field-based trauma care and medical coordination during the critical hours following a disaster—before formal hospital systems can absorb the patient load. Our teams include licensed physicians, nurses, paramedics, combat medics, and mental health professionals who establish forward triage sites, manage mass casualty incidents, and coordinate patient transfer to definitive care.

How we do it

  • Forward triage site establishment based on standard EMS mass casualty protocols.
  • Hemorrhage control, airway management, fracture stabilization, and wound care.
  • Medication administration within field-authorized scope of practice.
  • Coordination with local, state, and federal public health authorities during disease-risk events.
  • Medical needs assessment and patient census reporting to EOC partners.
  • Patient transfer coordination with receiving hospitals and EMS systems.
  • Provide a standardized first-aid/basic medical needs kit for quick distribution and delivery.

What your donation funds

Your donation provides supplies, equipment, and training that allows IRG's Medical Section to rapidly respond and complete the overall goal—saving lives.
