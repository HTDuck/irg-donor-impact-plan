Public Relations Section

Transparency through communication, across all teams.

06

What we do

IRG's Public Relations Section is the public face of the organization. By managing public affairs, donor relations, media coordination, and community outreach, this team builds the trust, credibility, and brand identity that allows IRG to complete the mission.

How we do it

  • Pre-deployment public briefings and community outreach messaging.
  • Real-time social media updates and field documentation during active missions.
  • Coordinate Traditional and Non-traditional media relations.
  • Post-deployment donor impact reports and partner agency debriefs.
  • Grant research, proposal writing, and compliance reporting for institutional funders.
  • Brand management: consistent visual identity across all IRG platforms and materials.
  • Volunteer recruitment campaigns and section-specific outreach.
