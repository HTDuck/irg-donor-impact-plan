# Executive Summary

Integrated Response Group is a veteran-founded 501(c)(3) nonprofit that deploys structured, military-grade disaster response across six integrated operational sections.

Canonical page: https://htduck.github.io/irg-donor-impact-plan/executive-summary/

Integrated Response Group (IRG) is a veteran-founded 501(c)(3) nonprofit that deploys structured, military-grade disaster response across six integrated operational sections: Command and Control, Crisis Intelligence Management, Medical, Logistics, Aviation, and Public Relations. Each section covers a critical capability—from satellite communications and aerial reconnaissance to field medicine, supply chain management, and public affairs—forming a complete system that can mobilize and sustain operations where standard emergency infrastructure has failed.

Unlike ad hoc relief efforts, IRG brings a command structure, interoperability with FEMA and partner agencies, and proprietary digital tools that create a common operating picture across all responding organizations. Our personnel are trained, credentialed, and mission-ready before disaster strikes—enabling rapid deployment within hours of activation.

This Donor Impact Plan provides a transparent, section-by-section breakdown of exactly where your contribution goes—which team it equips, what that team does, and what your donation accomplishes on the ground. Whether your gift funds a helicopter rescue sortie, a forward triage site, or a satellite command post, you will know precisely how it was used.

IRG is building a national-scale disaster response capability grounded in accountability, precision, and partnership. Every dollar contributed is a force multiplier in disaster zones where time and readiness save lives—and every section of this plan shows you exactly how.
