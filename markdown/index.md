# Donor Impact Plan

Subtitle: Operational Funding Guide

Founded and led by veterans. Built for the communities that need us most.

_Precision in Chaos, Unity in Response_

This Donor Impact Plan provides a transparent, section-by-section breakdown of exactly where your contribution goes.

Canonical page: https://htduck.github.io/irg-donor-impact-plan/

## A message to our donors

Every disaster is a test—of infrastructure, of coordination, and of the human will to respond. At IRG, we have seen what happens when the response is slow, fragmented, or under-resourced: lives are lost that did not need to be. We exist to close that gap. Thank you for being part of this mission.

## Operational sections

- [Command & Control](https://htduck.github.io/irg-donor-impact-plan/sections/command-and-control/): The nervous system of every IRG deployment.
- [Crisis Intelligence Management](https://htduck.github.io/irg-donor-impact-plan/sections/crisis-intelligence-management/): Turning chaos into actionable data.
- [Medical](https://htduck.github.io/irg-donor-impact-plan/sections/medical/): Field medicine in affected areas.
- [Logistics](https://htduck.github.io/irg-donor-impact-plan/sections/logistics/): Good intentions fail without logistics—we ensure their success.
- [Aviation](https://htduck.github.io/irg-donor-impact-plan/sections/aviation/): Reaching what ground teams cannot.
- [Public Relations](https://htduck.github.io/irg-donor-impact-plan/sections/public-relations/): Transparency through communication, across all teams.
