# Command & Control

_The nervous system of every IRG deployment._

Satellite comms, digital command infrastructure, and real-time situational awareness.

Canonical page: https://htduck.github.io/irg-donor-impact-plan/sections/command-and-control/

## What we do

IRG's Command and Control Section is the operational backbone of every mission. This team maintains uninterrupted communication, mobile command infrastructure, and the real-time situational awareness that keeps every section aligned. In disaster zones, communications infrastructure is often the first casualty—Command and Control Section bridges the gap until restoration.

## How we do it

- Satellite internet deployment (Starlink, etc.) at field command posts and staging areas.
- Digital App Chat for instant communications via text, voice, or video across multiple platforms.
- Radio network setup across multiple bands.
- Proprietary integrated digital command dashboard to provide a common operating picture across responding organizations.
- Cybersecurity and COMSEC protocols to protect sensitive operational data.
- Field computing: ruggedized laptops, tablets, encrypted drives, and mobile hotspots.
- Power systems: generators, solar charging kits, and battery banks for off-grid stability.
- Integration with ATAK, Maptician, and partner agency communication platforms.

## What your donation funds

Your donation funds satellite systems, radios, power equipment, and field computing tools that enable rapid deployment of our team. Without this core infrastructure, there is no organized response.
